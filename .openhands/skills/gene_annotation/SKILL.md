# Gene Annotation

Normalize the gene identifier and species, then organize available evidence for gene identity, aliases, function, expression, pathways, phenotypes, disease associations, orthologs, and database cross-references.

Mark facts supplied by the user separately from database-dependent claims. Do not invent Ensembl, NCBI Gene, HPA, GO, Reactome, GWAS, or disease-association results.
