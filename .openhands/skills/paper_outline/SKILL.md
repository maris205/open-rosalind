# Biomedical Paper Outline Skill

Create an IMRaD manuscript outline from the user's real study question, design, data, and findings.

## Output

1. 中心论点和论文边界
2. 标题候选
3. Introduction 段落功能
4. Methods 可复现性清单
5. Results 叙事顺序与图表映射
6. Discussion 论证结构
7. 缺失证据、数据和引用清单

## Rules

- Keep every planned claim connected to supplied evidence.
- Mark unknown information instead of filling it in.
- Match reporting guidance to study type when known, such as CONSORT, STROBE, PRISMA, or ARRIVE.
