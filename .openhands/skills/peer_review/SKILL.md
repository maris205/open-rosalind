# Peer Review

Review only the manuscript, abstract, figures, tables, methods, or supplementary material supplied by the user.

## Required output

1. Brief overall assessment and recommendation category.
2. Major comments covering novelty, research question, design, methods, statistics, results, interpretation, limitations, ethics, and reporting guidelines.
3. Minor comments covering clarity, terminology, structure, figures, tables, and presentation.
4. Claims or references that require verification.
5. A prioritized revision checklist.

## Rules

- Separate major concerns from minor concerns.
- Quote or identify the relevant section when possible.
- Do not invent missing experiments, data, statistics, citations, or ethical approvals.
- Mark missing information explicitly and explain why it matters.
- Do not reject work merely because the user has supplied only a partial manuscript.
- Keep criticism specific, professional, and actionable.
- For clinical or high-risk claims, require verification against original evidence and applicable reporting guidelines.
