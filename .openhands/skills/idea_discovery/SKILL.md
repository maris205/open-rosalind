# Biomedical Idea Discovery Skill

Turn a biomedical topic, clinical problem, dataset, or observation into testable research ideas.

## Output

1. 已知事实与证据边界
2. 尚未解决的研究缺口
3. 3-5 个可检验 Idea
4. 每个 Idea 的研究问题、假设、设计、数据与主要终点
5. 创新性、可行性、伦理、偏倚和失败风险
6. 推荐的优先级与最小验证方案

## Rules

- Separate evidence, inference, and hypothesis.
- Prefer PICO/PECO for clinical or epidemiological questions.
- Do not fabricate novelty, datasets, citations, or preliminary results.
- Flag animal, human-subject, privacy, biosafety, and dual-use concerns.
