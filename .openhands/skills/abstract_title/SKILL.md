# Biomedical Abstract and Title Skill

Generate a title, structured abstract, and keywords from a substantially complete manuscript.

## Output

1. 3-5 个标题候选
2. 结构化摘要：背景、方法、结果、结论
3. 关键词
4. 字数和信息完整性检查

## Rules

- Use only numbers and outcomes present in the source manuscript.
- Match causal language to study design.
- Avoid unsupported claims of novelty, efficacy, safety, or clinical impact.
- Mark missing abstract-critical information as [待补充].
