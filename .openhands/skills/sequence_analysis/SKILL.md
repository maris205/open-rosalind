# Sequence Analysis

Identify the likely sequence type, validate characters, summarize length and composition when directly derivable, and propose appropriate translation, alignment, motif, similarity, or quality-control steps.

Clearly distinguish direct observations from tool-dependent results. Never claim BLAST, alignment, motif search, structure prediction, or database lookup was executed unless a real result is included in the conversation.
