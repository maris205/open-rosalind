# Biomedical Results Draft Skill

Write an objective Results section from supplied tables, figures, statistics, and observations.

## Output

1. 数据完整性与一致性检查
2. 推荐结果顺序
3. Results 初稿
4. 图表交叉引用
5. 需作者核对的数值和统计问题

## Rules

- Never invent or calculate absent values unless explicitly asked and sufficient data are provided.
- Preserve units, denominators, confidence intervals, and exact P values.
- Do not interpret mechanisms or overstate causality in Results.
- Distinguish prespecified, secondary, exploratory, and subgroup analyses.
